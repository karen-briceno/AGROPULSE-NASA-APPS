## NASA & U.S. Federal Agency Datasets

### NASA Data

- **NASA Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 (HLS) v2.0**  
  Produced by NASA Goddard Space Flight Center (GSFC) and NASA Marshall Space Flight Center (MSFC); distributed through NASA LP DAAC.  
  **Use:** Satellite imagery and vegetation/heat-map analysis.  
  **Availability:** Openly available under NASA Earthdata data-use and citation guidance.

- **NASA SMAP L3/L4 Soil Moisture**  
  NASA Soil Moisture Active Passive (SMAP) Mission, NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL); distributed through NASA NSIDC DAAC.  
  **Use:** Soil moisture context and agricultural water-stress analysis.  
  **Availability:** Openly available under NASA Earthdata data-use and citation guidance.

- **NASA GPM IMERG / Gridded Meteorological Baseline (1981–2025)**  
  NASA Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) Mission; distributed through NASA GES DISC.  
  **Use:** Precipitation, climate baseline and long-term meteorological context.  
  **Availability:** Openly available under NASA Earthdata data-use and citation guidance.

### U.S. Federal Agency Data

- **USDA NRCS SSURGO Soil Survey**  
  U.S. Department of Agriculture, Natural Resources Conservation Service.  
  **Use:** Soil texture, water retention and soil composition data.  
  **Availability:** U.S. Government work; used in accordance with applicable USDA terms.

- **USDA NASS Cropland Data Layer (CDL), 2016–2025**  
  U.S. Department of Agriculture, National Agricultural Statistics Service; accessed through CropScape.  
  **Use:** Historical crop rotation and cropland-cover information.  
  **Availability:** U.S. Government work; used in accordance with applicable USDA terms.

- **USGS 3DEP 1/3 Arc-Second DEM**  
  U.S. Geological Survey.  
  **Use:** Elevation, terrain and slope analysis.  
  **Availability:** U.S. Government work; used in accordance with applicable USGS terms.
